  2. Growing up and changing views - 09.24

    Was never really a fan of going to hospitals unless I had to. Especially after my suspected shoulder tear and taking MRI, it only fueled my saltiness. I used to not believe in pain medication. I was not aware that they actually reduce the inflammations. Since I blow my back alot due to disk issue, after 2 years of back pain, I decided to give ibuprofen a shot as a last resort as I had issues walking in the morning not even training. It worked like magic, I remember the sensation. PT mentioned that the ibuprofen helped the chronic inflamation that I had or smth like that. Maybe it's time to do the same with Drs. Maybe I should take MRI on my back and see if there is smth they can do.

  5. Losing the fear of getting injured - 09.22

    I always had this fear of getting my ACL blown when performing Tai-Otoshi . This kinda sucked as a Korean lad myself, I can't perform my countryman's tokuiwaza. As I am learning more and more about myself, I realized that I need to conquer these fears no matter how small they are. So recently, I have been performing this throw, almost in a way that I am exposing my knee so that it is easy to get blown. Note that I am still being mindful for my partners, where I am staying low and below their knees, so that THEY will not get injured. It works great. This is what they mean by making deals with the devils huh.
