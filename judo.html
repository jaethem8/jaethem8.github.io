  1. Losing the fear of getting injured

    I always had this fear of getting my ACL blown when performing Tai-Otoshi. Probably after seeing some of my teammates knee got blown. I guess in a way, I am more scared of the medical bills then actual injury. As I am learning more and more about myself, I realized that I need to conquer these fears no matter how small they are. So recently, I have been performing this throw, almost in a way that I am exposing my knee so that it is easy to get blown. Note that I am still being mindful for my partners, where I am staying low and below their knees, so that THEY will not get injured. Anyways, It works now. At this point, with amount of throws I got, even if I get injured I probably would not regeret. Lessons learned.
