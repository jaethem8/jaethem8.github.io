  1. Losing the fear of getting injured - 09.22

    I always had this fear of getting my ACL blown when performing Tai-Otoshi . This kinda sucked as a Korean lad myself, I can't perform my countryman's tokuiwaza. As I am learning more and more about myself, I realized that I need to conquer these fears no matter how small they are. So recently, I have been performing this throw, almost in a way that I am exposing my knee so that it is easy to get blown. Note that I am still being mindful for my partners, where I am staying low and below their knees, so that THEY will not get injured. It works great. This is what they mean by making deals with the devils huh. At this point tho, the amount of throw I got, wouldn't care if ACL gets blown.
