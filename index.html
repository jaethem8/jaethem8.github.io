About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Iowa State University advised by Drs. Andrew Miner and Gianfranco Ciardo. I research decision diagrams and related applications in a broader field of formal methods. One day I hope to claim computability and complextiy as I decided to pursue graduate study after taking Dr. Andrei Migunov's Theory of Computation at Drake University. The graduate version of this course, also named Theory of Computation taught by Andrei's advisor Jack Lutz at Iowa State, is also equally life changing. However, I do have to note that my knowledge in such topic is still at its infant, if I am being generous.
